    Harborline Brands

    FP&A planning model for a mid-size consumer goods company: monthly close, annual budget, rolling forecast, reported in local currency and USD. Everything below was read from the live server.

    9 cubes19 dimensions8 rule cubes55 rule statements32 feeders33 processes1 chore

    Model tour

    For an analyst seeing this model for the first time.

    Harborline sells 40 SKUs under 12 brands in 7 countries through four channels. Finance loads units sold into the Sales cube and everything else about revenue is calculated: price comes from an annual Price List, cost from Product Cost, and discounts, returns and freight from rates in Assumptions.

    Payroll is driven, not loaded. Headcount holds FTE and salary by cost center; benefits and bonus rates come from Regional Assumptions by country. Other operating expenses are loaded from the GL straight into the P&L.

    The P&L pulls it together per cost center: revenue lines from Sales for the cost center's country, personnel lines from Headcount, a sales commission for Sales-function cost centers, and a shared-services allocation spread by headcount share. Local currency is translated to USD with FX Rates. The Control cube holds the current month, the forecast start, and the allocation switch.

    Months through 202608 are closed. 202609 is the open month. Forecast equals Actual for closed months and uses Budget plus an uplift for open months.

    Sales — forecast units, in plain English

    Closed months copy Actual. Open months take Budget units, add the uplift from Assumptions, and, for a SKU that replaced another, add the predecessor's budget. Inactive SKUs forecast zero.

    # Rolling forecast: closed months = Actual; open months = Budget × uplift,
# plus the predecessor's budgeted volume when this SKU replaced another one.
['Forecast', 'Units'] = N:
    IF(ATTRS('Time', !Time, 'Status') @= 'Closed', ['Actual', 'Units'],
    IF(ATTRS('Product', !Product, 'Active') @= 'N', 0,
       ['Budget', 'Units'] * (1 + DB('Assumptions', 'Forecast', !Time, !Region, !Channel, 'Forecast Uplift %'))
       + IF(ATTRS('Product', !Product, 'Predecessor SKU') @= '', 0,
            DB('Sales', 'Budget', !Time, !Region, !Channel, ATTRS('Product', !Product, 'Predecessor SKU'), 'Units'))));
    Show the matching feeders 
    ['Budget', 'Units']   => ['Forecast', 'Units'], ['Actual vs Budget', 'Units'], ['Forecast vs Budget', 'Units'],
                         DB('Sales', 'Forecast', !Time, !Region, !Channel, ATTRS('Product', !Product, 'Successor SKU'), 'Units');
# The rule reads Predecessor SKU; the feeder reads Successor SKU. The two attributes must stay in sync.

    P&L — shared-services allocation

    Pool cost centers push their allocable costs out. Each operating cost center takes the pool total in USD times its headcount share, converted back to its own currency.

    ['Allocation In', 'Local']  = N: IF(ATTRS('Cost Center', !Cost Center, 'Receives Allocation') @= 'Y',
    DB('P&L', !Version, !Time, 'Shared Services', 'Allocable Costs', 'USD')
    * DB('Headcount', !Version, !Time, !Cost Center, 'All Job Bands', 'Headcount Share')
    \ DB('FX Rates', !Version, !Time, ATTRS('Cost Center', !Cost Center, 'Currency'), 'Average Rate'), 0);
    Show the matching feeder, and why it is flagged 
    ['Salaries', 'Local'] => DB(IF(ATTRS('Cost Center', !Cost Center, 'Function') @= 'Shared Services'
                              & DB('Control', !Version, 'Allocations Enabled') = 1, 'P&L', ''),
                            !Version, !Time, 'Operating Cost Centers', 'Allocation In', 'Local');

    The feeder has a condition the rule does not: it checks Control / Allocations Enabled. If that flag is 0 the rule still calculates at every leaf, but nothing is fed, so the consolidations show zero. Worth knowing before a close.
